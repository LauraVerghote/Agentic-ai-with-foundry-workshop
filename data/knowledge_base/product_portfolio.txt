Product: NovaRelief 200mg / 400mg Tablets
-----------------------------------------
Generic Name: Novaprofen
Therapeutic Area: Pain Management
ATC Code: M01AE90
Marketing Authorization: EU/1/2019/001
Authorization Date: March 15, 2019
Marketing Authorization Holder: NovaPharma NV, Brussels, Belgium

Indication: Treatment of mild to moderate pain and inflammation associated with osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, and acute musculoskeletal disorders in adults.

Dosage and Administration:
- Osteoarthritis: 200mg twice daily with food. May increase to 400mg twice daily based on clinical response.
- Rheumatoid arthritis: 400mg twice daily with food.
- Acute musculoskeletal pain: 400mg initially, then 200mg every 8 hours. Maximum duration: 7 days.
- Maximum daily dose: 800mg.
- Elderly patients (over 65): Start at lowest dose (200mg twice daily). Monitor renal function.
- Renal impairment (mild, GFR 60-89): No dose adjustment. Moderate (GFR 30-59): Reduce to 200mg once daily. Severe (GFR below 30): Contraindicated.

Contraindications:
- Known hypersensitivity to novaprofen or any excipient
- Active gastrointestinal bleeding or perforation
- Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C)
- Severe renal impairment (GFR below 30 mL/min)
- Third trimester of pregnancy
- History of NSAID-induced asthma
- Active inflammatory bowel disease
- Severe heart failure (NYHA Class IV)

Common Adverse Reactions (frequency 1-10%):
- Gastrointestinal: nausea, dyspepsia, abdominal pain, diarrhea
- Nervous system: headache, dizziness
- Skin: rash

Serious Adverse Reactions (frequency less than 1%):
- Gastrointestinal bleeding or perforation
- Cardiovascular thrombotic events
- Severe skin reactions (Stevens-Johnson syndrome)
- Hepatotoxicity
- Renal failure

Storage: Store below 25 degrees Celsius. Keep in original packaging to protect from moisture.
Shelf Life: 36 months.
Pack Sizes: 30 tablets, 60 tablets, 90 tablets.

Product: CardioShield 5mg / 10mg Film-Coated Tablets
-----------------------------------------------------
Generic Name: Novazartan
Therapeutic Area: Cardiovascular
ATC Code: C09CA90
Marketing Authorization: EU/1/2020/045
Authorization Date: June 22, 2020
Marketing Authorization Holder: NovaPharma NV, Brussels, Belgium

Indication: Treatment of essential hypertension in adults. Treatment of heart failure (NYHA Class II-III) in adults when ACE inhibitors are not tolerated.

Dosage and Administration:
- Hypertension: Starting dose 5mg once daily. May increase to 10mg once daily after 4 weeks if blood pressure not adequately controlled.
- Heart failure: Starting dose 5mg once daily. Titrate to 10mg once daily as tolerated. Monitor potassium and renal function at 1, 3, and 6 months.
- Hepatic impairment (mild to moderate): Starting dose 5mg. Do not exceed 5mg daily. Severe hepatic impairment: Contraindicated.
- No dose adjustment needed for mild to moderate renal impairment. Severe renal impairment (GFR below 30): Not recommended.
- Must not be co-administered with aliskiren in diabetic patients.

Contraindications:
- Known hypersensitivity to novazartan or excipients
- Second and third trimester of pregnancy
- Severe hepatic impairment or biliary cirrhosis
- Concomitant use with aliskiren in patients with diabetes or renal impairment (GFR below 60)

Common Adverse Reactions (frequency 1-10%):
- Dizziness, especially during first days of treatment
- Hypotension (more common in heart failure patients)
- Hyperkalemia
- Fatigue

Drug Interactions:
- Potassium-sparing diuretics: Risk of hyperkalemia. Monitor potassium levels.
- Lithium: Increased lithium levels. Monitor lithium concentration.
- NSAIDs: May reduce antihypertensive effect and increase renal risk.

Storage: Store below 30 degrees Celsius. No special storage conditions required.
Shelf Life: 24 months.
Pack Sizes: 28 tablets, 56 tablets, 98 tablets.

Product: ImmunoBoost 50mg Solution for Injection
-------------------------------------------------
Generic Name: Novamab
Therapeutic Area: Immunology
ATC Code: L04AA90
Marketing Authorization: EU/1/2022/112
Authorization Date: November 8, 2022
Marketing Authorization Holder: NovaPharma NV, Brussels, Belgium

Indication: Treatment of moderate to severe plaque psoriasis in adults who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy. Treatment of active psoriatic arthritis in adults who have had an inadequate response to disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs).

Dosage and Administration:
- Plaque psoriasis: 50mg subcutaneous injection at Weeks 0, 4, and every 12 weeks thereafter. Administered by healthcare professional or self-injection after training.
- Psoriatic arthritis: 50mg subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks. May be administered in combination with methotrexate.
- Injection sites: Thigh, abdomen (avoiding 5cm around navel), or upper arm. Rotate injection sites.
- Missed dose: Administer as soon as possible, then resume regular schedule. Do not double the dose.
- Treatment should be initiated and supervised by a physician experienced in the diagnosis and treatment of the indicated conditions.

Contraindications:
- Known hypersensitivity to novamab or excipients
- Active tuberculosis or other severe infections
- Live vaccines: Wait at least 4 weeks before and 12 weeks after treatment

Special Warnings:
- Screen for tuberculosis before initiation. Monitor for signs of active TB during treatment.
- Evaluate patients for hepatitis B before starting treatment.
- Monitor for signs of infection during and after treatment. Discontinue if serious infection develops.
- Assess for malignancy risk. Caution in patients with history of malignancy.

Common Adverse Reactions (frequency 1-10%):
- Injection site reactions (redness, swelling, pain)
- Upper respiratory tract infections
- Headache
- Fatigue

Storage: Store in a refrigerator (2 to 8 degrees Celsius). Do not freeze. Keep in outer carton to protect from light. May be stored at room temperature (up to 25 degrees Celsius) for a single period of up to 14 days.
Shelf Life: 18 months when refrigerated.
Pack Sizes: 1 pre-filled syringe, 2 pre-filled syringes.
