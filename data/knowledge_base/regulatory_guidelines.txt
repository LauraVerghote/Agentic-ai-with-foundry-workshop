1. Regulatory Submission Requirements
--------------------------------------

1.1 Variation Submissions (Type IA, IB, Type II)

Type IA Variations (Minor, no prior approval needed):
- Notification within 12 months of implementation
- Examples: Change in name/address of MAH, minor change to product information text
- Required documents: Application form, updated SmPC/PIL sections, cover letter
- Fee: EUR 2,500 per variation

Type IB Variations (Minor, notify and implement):
- Submit notification, implement after 30 days if no objection
- Examples: Minor formulation change, tightening of specification limits
- Required documents: Application form, justification, updated specifications, stability data if applicable
- Fee: EUR 4,000 per variation

Type II Variations (Major):
- Requires prior approval before implementation
- Assessment timeline: 60 days (standard), 90 days for complex safety changes
- Examples: New indication, change in dosing, significant formulation change
- Required documents: Full variation dossier including updated Module 2 summaries, clinical/non-clinical data as needed
- Fee: EUR 15,000 per variation

1.2 Periodic Safety Update Reports (PSURs)

PSUR submission frequency by product:
- NovaRelief: Every 12 months (next due: March 15, 2025)
- CardioShield: Every 12 months (next due: June 22, 2025)
- ImmunoBoost: Every 6 months (next due: May 8, 2025)

PSUR content requirements:
- Worldwide exposure data
- Summary of safety concerns from the Risk Management Plan
- Signal detection and evaluation
- Benefit-risk assessment
- Proposed risk minimization measures

PSUR preparation timeline:
- Data lock point: As per EU birth date
- Draft to Safety Committee: 30 days after data lock
- Internal review: 15 days
- Submission deadline: 70 days after data lock point (for EU single assessment)

1.3 Risk Management Plans (RMPs)

All products must maintain current RMPs. RMP updates required when:
- New safety concern identified
- Change in benefit-risk profile
- New risk minimization measure proposed
- PSUR assessment recommends update
- Submission of Type II variation affecting safety

RMP structure follows EU GVP Module V format:
- Part I: Product overview
- Part II: Safety specification (non-clinical, clinical trial, post-marketing)
- Part III: Pharmacovigilance plan
- Part IV: Plans for post-authorization efficacy studies
- Part V: Risk minimization measures
- Part VI: Summary of the RMP
- Part VII: Annexes

2. Pharmacovigilance Requirements
-----------------------------------

2.1 Adverse Event Reporting Timelines

Individual Case Safety Reports (ICSRs):
- Serious, unexpected adverse reactions: 15 calendar days from awareness
- Fatal or life-threatening unexpected reactions: 7 calendar days initial report, 15 days for follow-up
- Non-serious adverse reactions: 90 calendar days
- Reports from literature: 15 days for serious, 90 days for non-serious

2.2 Signal Detection

Routine signal detection activities:
- Review of EudraVigilance data: Monthly
- Disproportionality analysis: Quarterly
- Literature screening (PubMed, Embase): Weekly
- Social media monitoring: Not required per current SOP but under evaluation

Signal validation criteria:
- New adverse reaction not in current SmPC
- Increase in severity or frequency of known reaction
- New at-risk population identified
- New drug interaction identified

2.3 Safety Communication

Dear Healthcare Professional Communications (DHPCs):
- Required when urgent safety information must be communicated
- Approval by national competent authority before distribution
- Distribution within 24 hours of approval
- Channels: Direct mail, email to registered HCPs, publication on company website

3. Quality and Manufacturing Compliance
-----------------------------------------

3.1 Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) Requirements

Annual product quality reviews must include:
- Batch analysis trending (all critical quality attributes)
- Stability data review
- Deviation and CAPA summary
- Complaint trending
- Change control summary
- Supplier qualification status

3.2 Stability Testing Program

Long-term stability: 25 degrees C / 60% RH, testing at 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36 months
Accelerated stability: 40 degrees C / 75% RH, testing at 0, 1, 3, 6 months
Photostability: Per ICH Q1B guideline

Stability commitments per product:
- NovaRelief: 3 production batches per year (ongoing)
- CardioShield: 3 production batches per year (ongoing)
- ImmunoBoost: 1 batch per year (post-approval commitment until 2027)

3.3 Batch Release

Batch release checklist:
- Certificate of Analysis (CoA) review and approval
- Batch manufacturing record review
- Deviation assessment (no open critical deviations)
- Stability trending within specification
- Qualified Person (QP) declaration
- Artwork and labeling verification

4. Product Information Updates
-------------------------------

4.1 Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC)

SmPC updates must be submitted when:
- New clinical data affects prescribing information
- PSUR assessment identifies new safety information
- Signal assessment results in labeling change
- Regulatory authority request

SmPC change process:
1. Identify need for change (signal, PSUR, clinical study)
2. Draft proposed text changes (tracked changes format)
3. Medical review by Chief Medical Officer
4. Regulatory assessment of variation type required
5. Legal review of patient information leaflet changes
6. Submit variation to EMA or national authority
7. Implement approved changes within 6 months of approval

4.2 Package Leaflet (PIL) Requirements

Patient information leaflets must:
- Be written at a reading level accessible to general public
- Include all mandatory sections per EU legislation
- Be user-tested with target patient population
- Be available in all official languages of member states where marketed
- Be updated simultaneously with SmPC changes
